## What changed, and what it means

- **Reviewer agreement is now high.** Every lesson job got three rejects. Across controls, the same claims fail over and over: switch c.setup, c.fall and c.clang failed 12 of 12 times; one-byte c.fprint 10 of 10; factorial c.order 10 of 10. That's what the stored passages and rubric v4 were for (A3), and it worked. The remaining split: on each lesson, the three lesson reviewers disagree on 1 to 8 secondary items.
- **But the controls now fail on real wording defects, not noise.** The text is wrong in ways the stricter rubric catches. So reviewer reliability is still not demonstrated: a 95% control rate can't be measured while the clean text itself fails.

## The defects (exact fix list for one more Track A round)

**1. Scope (most of the failures).**
- Rubric v4 (A3) says a sentence that names no program must hold for every program in the job.
- The jobs include the variant programs used for value mutations: fact_n1/2/4/5, views_200/m1/321 and neuron_k1/2/3. A2 also added into_break.c and with_break.c.
- So unscoped claims are now false somewhere:
  - **switch:** c.setup, c.fall
  - **one-byte:** c.fprint, c.setup, c.dbits
  - **factorial:** c.setup, c.depth, c.ret3, c.main, c.order
  - **neuron:** c.round, c.add
- **Fix:** each names its program ("In factorial.c, …"). A3 should have audited every claim against the new rule. It didn't, and I didn't ask it to.

**2. Evidence missing from the job (format bugs, not wording).**
- switch c.clang: clang diagnostics are captured but not put into the job (12 of 12).
- neuron c.kformula: the explorer's slider ranges and step aren't in the job (7 of 9).
- **Fix:** the job carries the clang diagnostics and the explorer's control spec as facts.

**3. Single wording errors.**
- switch c.warn says "a warning" where gcc-16 gives two.
- switch c.break: "Here that is the switch" on standalone cards.
- one-byte c.recap drops "on this machine".
- factorial: t.216 "The next scene answers this question" fails in transcript.txt, which never states line 4.
- factorial: the m.base wrong belief is true for fact_n1.c.
- factorial c.frame: justification drops the non-VLA qualifier.

None of these are new kinds of error. Each is small and mechanical.
