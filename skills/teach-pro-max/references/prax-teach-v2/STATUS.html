What “ready” means

Ready requires the final candidate to pass the unchanged engineering contract, exact-payload reviews, held-out teaching comparisons, rendered inspection, trusted full verification, and scoped packaging gate. Acceptance and reversible installation must be recorded externally. It does not mean “best tutor,” “proven to teach better,” “field accessible,” or “automatic replacement for prax-teach.” Those labels remain blocked until the corresponding evidence gates pass.
