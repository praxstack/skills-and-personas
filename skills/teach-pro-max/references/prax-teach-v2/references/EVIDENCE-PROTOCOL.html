Visual runtime evidence protocol

Engineering evidence records exact bytes, command, toolchain, result, and limitation. Synthetic fixtures are labelled synthetic; agent/browser checks are engineering; neither is learner evidence.

The external protocol is predeclared: (1) representative keyboard, screen-reader, zoom/reflow, and reduced-motion field walkthrough; (2) immediate retrieval/explanation/application/discrimination tasks; (3) the same concept after an elapsed 7–14 day delay; and (4) a structurally novel transfer item. Record participant consent, task version, assistance/hint level, uncertainty, missing data, and integrity hashes. Do not substitute a planned delay, generated data, or an automated accessibility result.
