Mission: independently diagnose slow database workloads, choose proportionate fixes, and verify improvements with measurements.

| Week | Focus | Evidence of progress |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Measurement, latency, `EXPLAIN`/`EXPLAIN ANALYZE` | Baseline and explain a slow query |
| 2 | Indexes and access paths | Design and test composite, partial, and covering indexes |
| 3 | Query planning and joins | Fix cardinality, sargability, and N+1 problems |
| 4 | Transactions and concurrency | Diagnose locks, MVCC issues, deadlocks, and pool pressure |
| 5 | Workload and production tuning | Use workload metrics, statistics, caching, and safe rollout |
| 6 | Capstone investigation | Diagnose, fix, benchmark, and defend a complete optimization |

Each week includes one bounded lesson, hands-on practice, retrieval review, and a short teach-back. The final review targets retention over 30 days.
