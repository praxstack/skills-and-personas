## Why no lesson verifies

The controls fail on real wording, not reviewer noise:
- **Scope, most of it.**
  - Rubric v4 says a sentence that names no program must hold for every program in the job.
  - The jobs include variant programs: fact_n1/2/4/5, views_200/m1/321, neuron_k1/2/3, plus switch's with_break and into_break.
  - Unscoped claims ("returns 6 to main", "prints 66.000000", "z prints 0.6", "case 2 has no break") are false for some of them.
- **New errors introduced in Track B:**
  - one-byte calls `(double)a` "a new object" (a cast gives a value; the object is `d`), in a claim and on the drawn label;
  - neuron's 1e-16 note says 0.8 × 1.5 is "rounded to the nearest double" and alone causes the tiny z. It is an exact binary64 tie that rounds to even, and z still misses 0 at three of four settings with 1.2 stored;
  - switch narration says "GCC" where the evidence is gcc-16 only. B5 changed it to dodge the spoken-number rule, so two checks pulled against each other;
  - the neuron IVE points to the wrong time for its answer.
- **Job-format gaps:**
  - clang diagnostics are captured but not put in the job;
  - the explorer's slider ranges are not in the job.
- **Transcript-only breakage:** factorial's "The next scene answers this question" fails in transcript.txt.
