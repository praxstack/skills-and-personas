## Why these scores

**Stills:** legible hierarchy, soft role colours and diagrams that do explanatory work. Voice separates event roles from grammatical position and shows both forms. Base rates retains both populations, both kinds of flags and the changed denominator; its proportional bar gives a useful second representation. Diffusion makes opposing traffic and the net difference readable, with expected-count/model limits rather than claiming tracked particles. They are clean and useful, although less tightly integrated than Atlas: text below often repeats what the figure has already said, and diffusion arrows do not visually encode their differing magnitudes. Those weaknesses do not make the current numerical explanations false.

**Explorers:** content remains understandable and the simple controls do change the diagram. My Looks score is below target because the layout gives substantial desktop space to contents and notes while shrinking the mechanism into the center. The diagram's numeric and explanatory labels are visibly smaller than the neighboring prose; Atlas gives its mechanism a much stronger stage. Graph-paper lines, the red margin, handwritten blue controls, highlighting, badges and serif prose compete for attention. On mobile, the figure itself is readable once scrolled into view, but most of the opening viewport is title/chrome/context before reaching that figure. This is a concrete presentation weakness, not a correctness or mandatory-practice claim. I did not increase the score to satisfy the threshold.

**Sampled final video frames:** the mechanism has a larger stage than in the desktop explorer, with readable quantitative labels and matching narration captions at the rechecked states. The two previously contradictory transitions now settle correctly at the sampled times. The handwriting/grid treatment remains more visually busy than Atlas, and many scene changes rely on revised parameters plus narration rather than a more specific explanatory visual action. These sampled frames reach 8 in my judgment, but do not establish every frame's timing or the voice's clarity and prosody.
