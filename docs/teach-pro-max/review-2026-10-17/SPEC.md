# Completion specification

Preserve the approved general teacher objective in ../research-2026-10-16/PRODUCT-DIRECTION.md and implementation plan ../../superpowers/plans/2026-10-17-teacher-completion.md. This document records observable criteria, not new acceptance thresholds.

- WHEN teaching is requested, the skill SHALL choose available suitable media and explain mechanism, intermediate states and limitations, adapting until learner satisfaction or chosen stop.
- WHEN no style override exists, delivered teaching SHALL use AskTheMove paper/ink/coach hierarchy with accessible complete states and purposeful marks.
- WHEN ongoing course organization is useful, the skill SHALL distinguish course map, current teaching and durable notes without forcing setup for a short question.
- WHEN ordinary edit/delete/write intent is requested, routing SHALL preserve that intent without activating teaching or consent setup.
- WHEN choosing a packaged visual, routing SHALL read immutable bytes with bounded checks, never build or overwrite installed artifacts.
- WHEN current evidence is selected, all consumers SHALL verify the explicit exact-source binding and preserve historical proof against historical inventories.
- WHEN learning-state consent is absent, declined or withdrawn, personalization and state writes SHALL follow the existing protected consent contract. Save-nothing SHALL forbid durable lesson files too.
- WHEN full continuity reset occurs, host coordination SHALL cancel outstanding updates and reread after any explicit regrant; numeric revisions protect one record lifetime only.
- WHEN unchanged held-out, runtime, rendered and review gates all pass, the accepted revision SHALL be installed reversibly and consumer links/fresh use checked. Otherwise original installation SHALL remain.

No new app/framework, universal generator, sanitizer weakening, learner schema migration, remote publication, paid account or hidden evaluation change.
