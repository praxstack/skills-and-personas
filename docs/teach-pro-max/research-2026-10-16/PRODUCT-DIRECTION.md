# Teacher skill: user-confirmed product direction

Confirmed by Prax in this conversation on 2026-10-16. This records requirements and research direction, not a claim of implemented or evaluated functionality.

Build a harness-independent teacher skill that guides one session or coordinates a series of sessions in Codex, Claude, Pi, or another capable host. Discover and use the best suitable available tools for drawings, animations, interactive visualizations and videos. Select media for explanatory value: every visual explains the idea, including why and how. Teach across subjects and levels, from first intuition to deep technical understanding. Adapt explanations, examples and media when the learner remains confused. Continue learner-led exploration until the learner is satisfied or chooses to stop; a completed lesson or quiz score is not the finish line. Coordinate agents when the host supports it and doing so helps teaching. Testing supports this product and must not become the product.

Default aesthetics requested: Prax's AskTheMove / ChessLab design language, with hand-drawn live-whiteboard teaching. Preserve readability and learner overrides. Inspect actual source rather than assume an appearance from the name.

Research references requested: Brilliant (visualization and interaction), Educative (separate course details and notes), mattpocock/skills productivity/teach, Fanout, Nibble, Imprint, YouTube EP3DvnIkybo, AlgoCamp, AlgoMaster, roadmap.sh/inference-engineering, Wondering, DeepLearning.AI courses. The concatenated Imprint/roadmap URL is interpreted as two separate references. Borrow pedagogical patterns and interaction principles; do not represent copied third-party assets as original work.

New findings remain research proposals until integrated through the existing governed design workflow. This note does not rewrite frozen evaluation, extend its deadline, approve a pending runtime-receipt path, or prove installation complete.
