## More precise reusable patterns

**Mission and scope.** Pocock's [mission format](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mattpocock/skills/main/skills/productivity/teach/MISSION-FORMAT.md) records why the learner cares, observable success, constraints and exclusions. Adopt these lightweight fields, while accepting curiosity and deep intuition as valid goals. Do not force a practical deliverable or a preliminary interview when the user's aim is already clear. One mission per workspace is an organizational suggestion, not a reason to block interdisciplinary learning.

**Evidence-bearing notes.** The [learning-record format](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mattpocock/skills/main/skills/productivity/teach/LEARNING-RECORD-FORMAT.md) distinguishes demonstrated understanding, reported prior knowledge and corrected misconceptions from mere coverage. It permits evidence and supersession without demanding long session logs. Adopt concise records that preserve this distinction; record unresolved uncertainty rather than marking a concept mastered after exposure or one correct answer.

**Curated source notes.** The [resource format](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mattpocock/skills/main/skills/productivity/teach/RESOURCES-FORMAT.md) asks what each source covers and when to use it, surfaces gaps, and records preferences about communities. Adopt annotated provenance and gaps. Keep sources as references supporting teaching, rather than turning the lesson into a reading list.

**Intuition before formal expansion.** Official [Machine Learning Specialization](https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/machine-learning-specialization/) text retrieved through Exa describes visuals, implementation and optional mathematical explanation, with ungraded notebooks and interactive graphs. Inference: layer explanation so a learner can move between intuition, concrete execution and formal derivation; offer mathematical depth when it resolves uncertainty. Do not universally postpone math or assume viewing a visual establishes intuition.

**Prerequisite-aware progression.** Official [Machine Learning in Production](https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses/machine-learning-in-production) extraction identifies prior AI/deep-learning and Python experience and combines lectures, notes, labs and assessments. Inference: identify the minimum missing prerequisite and supply a local bridge; avoid assigning an entire preceding course when a small explanation suffices. The specialization page was subsequently fetched through Exa and confirmed the visual-to-code-to-optional-math sequence; the production course remains an official-page search extract. Neither is a full course audit.

**Honest tool orchestration.** Fanout's [Agent Control Room](https://fanout.sh/labs/agent-control-room) explicitly describes device-local modeled scenarios, bounded retries and estimated cost. Its [Model Router](https://fanout.sh/labs/model-router-and-pareto-explorer) separately describes a live catalog fetch triggered by a user action. Inference: label simulations, live observations and estimates distinctly. A teacher should choose a renderer/calculator/notebook only when its output clarifies the concept, preserve capability-independent lesson state, and disclose when a requested interaction cannot run.

## Proposed teaching loop — synthesis, not a source fact

1. Locate the learner's purpose, current explanation and specific point of confusion, using existing context first.
2. Build one causal account with a concrete example. Choose representations for their explanatory role: process trace for changing state; graph for dependencies; plot for quantitative relationships; geometry for space; contrast table for cases; manipulation for cause and effect. Avoid decorative variety.
3. Invite prediction, manipulation, explanation, comparison or a real task as useful diagnostic evidence. Quizzes are optional instruments.
4. If confusion remains, change the representation, example scale, analogy or prerequisite bridge. Explain analogy boundaries. Do not repeat the same wording louder.
5. Preserve a compact reference, source links and the learner's unresolved questions. Continue until the learner is satisfied, or explicitly pauses; never force completion because a score or predetermined step count was reached. Satisfaction is not proof of permanent retention, so notes should distinguish the two.
