The principal uncovered additions are the AskTheMove default and an explicit separate course-map/notes presentation contract. Cross-output visual-vocabulary parity and immediate finished-figure access would make the existing media policy more precise. These are source-text gaps only; implementing them remains separate from this research report. The synthesis explicitly preserves that boundary (**S:37–39**).
